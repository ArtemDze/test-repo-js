Studio

Craft on a living stage

Shape motifs with precise controls for geometry, folds, spacing, and color. Lock a seed, compare A and B, apply tonight’s cue, and open Premiere when the composition is ready.

  • Symmetry, layout, stroke, and kinetic motion
  • Programme notes written from your parameters
  • Export polished stills when you’re ready

Atelier

Motif library, ready for the stage

Browse curated starting compositions — Footlight Crown, Paper Masque, Quiet Vortex — then open any motif on the Studio stage with a single gesture.

  • Living kinetic previews
  • On Stage badge synced to your active motif

Labs

Train the eye before you craft

Eye drills first, practice second — learn to see folds, contrast, chaos, motion, and harmony. Field notes stay poetic; progress stays on your device.

  • A/B perception drills with reveal copy
  • Practice stages like Symmetry Chamber
  • Craft tips linked back to Studio
