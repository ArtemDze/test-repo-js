Privacy

We collect nothing

Effective date: August 3, 2026 · Last updated: August 3, 2026

Jestora Pattern Studio is a private creative stage. This Policy explains — clearly — that we do not collect personal data from the App, and how on-device craft works.

The short version

We collect nothing. No accounts. No analytics SDK. No ads. Your work stays on your device until you export it.
App Store–ready · On-device by design

Data we collect

None

The App does not transmit compositions, preferences, or identity to our servers.

Accounts

Not required

There is no sign-in, no email capture in the App, and no public profile.

Advertising

None

No third-party ad networks. We do not sell personal information.

1. Who we are

Jestora Pattern Studio (“the App”) is a creative iOS application for geometric composition, provided by the developer of Jestora Pattern Studio (“we”, “us”).

Developer: Matthew Mendoza
Privacy contact: matthewmendoza627@gmail.com

2. Our commitment in plain language

  • We collect nothing from the App — no telemetry of your compositions, no marketing analytics SDK, no account database.
  • No account is required. Open the App and create.
  • Compositions stay on your device in the App’s local storage until you delete them or delete the App.
  • No ads and no sale of personal information.
  • Exports are yours to share — only when you choose iOS share destinations.

3. Information stored on your device (not collected by us)

The App may store the following locally on your iPhone or iPad. This data is not uploaded to us as part of the standard App experience:

  • Compositions — titles, pattern parameters, optional notes, thumbnails, and favorites in Library.
  • Preferences — haptic feedback, motion intensity, default canvas ratio, and similar Stage Preferences.
  • Labs progress — completed eye drills and field-note checkmarks.
  • Onboarding / cue state — prologue completion and whether today’s creative cue was dismissed or applied.

Deleting the App removes this local App data from that device. You can also clear Library from Profile or delete individual compositions.

4. Information we do not collect

As provided in the current App experience, we do not:

  • Collect personal identifiers through the App
  • Create user accounts or require sign-in
  • Use third-party advertising or marketing analytics SDKs
  • Track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising
  • Collect precise location for advertising or analytics
  • Sell your information to data brokers
  • Run a social feed, cloud sync, or public gallery of your work

If this ever changes in a future version, we will update this Policy and, where required, provide notice and updated App Store privacy labels before collecting new data types.

5. Photos, exports, and sharing

If you export a composition, the image is generated on your device. If you share it, you choose the destination (Photos, Files, Messages, Mail, and so on) through Apple’s system share sheet. We do not upload your exports to our servers as part of the standard App flow.

6. Analytics, crash data, and diagnostics

The App is not built around a third-party marketing analytics SDK. Apple may process diagnostic or usage data if you have enabled relevant system options (for example App Analytics or analytics & improvements settings on your device). That processing is governed by Apple’s policies and your device settings — not by a Jestora backend.

7. Support email (optional contact you initiate)

If you email us for support or privacy questions, we receive the information you include in that message (such as your email address and any details you write). We use it only to respond and to keep ordinary correspondence records. We do not use support email to build advertising profiles.

8. Purchases

If the App Store listing includes a paid download or in-app purchases, payment processing is handled by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience creative tool. Because we do not collect personal information through the App, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children via the App. If a child contacts us by email, contact us and we will delete that correspondence where applicable.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • On device: local App data remains until you delete compositions, clear Library, reset the App, or delete the App.
  • Support email: retained as needed to respond and for ordinary business record-keeping.

11. Security

We rely on iOS sandboxing and on-device storage for App content. Keep your device passcode and software updates current. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we intentionally avoid cloud storage of your compositions.

12. International users

The App is distributed via the App Store. Optional support email may be processed where we operate. By contacting us, you understand that limited correspondence data may be handled in those locations.

13. Your choices

  • Adjust haptics and motion intensity in Profile.
  • Use system Reduce Motion to limit animation.
  • Delete compositions or clear Library on device.
  • Delete the App to remove local App data from that device.
  • Email privacy questions to the address above.

14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised Policy.
