FAQ

Where is my work stored?

On your device, in Library. Deleting the App removes local App data from that device.

Do I need an account?

No. Jestora Pattern Studio does not require sign-in. We collect nothing from the App.

How do I export?

From Studio, use Export to create a still, then share through the iOS share sheet.

Today’s cue keeps coming back

Apply or dismiss the cue to mark it done for the day. You can reopen it from the Studio menu anytime.

Privacy
